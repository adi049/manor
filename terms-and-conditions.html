1. Website information

This website provides general information about HOTEL ND MANOR in Mothrowala, Dehradun. Content and photographs are presented for reference. Room layouts, amenities, arrival arrangements and other operational details can change; confirm what applies to your dates directly with the hotel.

Nothing on this website guarantees that a particular room, facility or arrangement is available on a particular date.

2. Rooms, guest count and rates

The room categories displayed are Deluxe Room, Super Deluxe Room With Balcony, and Royal Suite With Bathtub. Room layouts, facilities and availability should be confirmed with HOTEL ND MANOR.

Contact the hotel for current rates. Any approved rate quote applies to two guests. Maximum occupancy is three guests per room, including adults and children. Additional-person charges apply; confirm the amount and applicable terms directly with the hotel. Availability, current rates and inclusions depend on the hotel's confirmation for your dates.

3. Banquet and event enquiries

The website describes an indoor, air-conditioned banquet hall and accepts enquiries about weddings, celebrations, family functions and private events. An enquiry does not reserve the hall. The hotel must confirm the date, suitability, capacity, layout, arrangements and any applicable prices directly.

No event capacity, package, menu inclusion or venue size is promised on this website.

4. Forms and direct enquiries

Forms on this site prepare a WhatsApp message draft using details you enter. They do not submit information to a HOTEL ND MANOR website database, collect payment or confirm a room or event. If you choose to continue, review the draft and press Send in WhatsApp yourself. The hotel will confirm availability, current rates and booking terms manually.

A booking or event is confirmed only when the hotel communicates its confirmation and the applicable terms directly to you.

5. Third-party platforms and links

Links to maps, social networks, WhatsApp and booking platforms lead to services operated by other organisations. Their availability, prices, policies, terms and information handling are controlled by those services. Review their terms before using them.

This website does not collect a payment or act as a booking platform.

6. Questions

For current room details, rates, event arrangements or a question about these website terms, contact HOTEL ND MANOR at ndmanorhotel@gmail.com or use the official phone and WhatsApp choices on this site.

These website terms do not replace a specific confirmation or terms provided directly by the hotel for an individual stay or event.
