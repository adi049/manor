Privacy policy.

How this static website handles enquiry details, browser storage and links to other services.

Last updated 19 October 2026

In brief

The website does not send form entries to a HOTEL ND MANOR website database. If you choose a WhatsApp number, your device opens a prefilled message draft. You decide whether to send it.

ContactWebsite and formsWhatsApp and external servicesBrowser storageRetention and requests

1. Who to contact

This website presents information about HOTEL ND MANOR in Mothrowala, Dehradun. For questions about information you choose to share with the hotel, email ndmanorhotel@gmail.com or use one of the listed hotel contact options.

2. Information entered on this website

The room and event enquiry forms may ask for your name, phone number, optional email address, enquiry type, room category, dates, guest counts and an optional message. The form code runs in your browser and prepares a message draft. This static website has no form-submission backend and does not store the submitted fields in a website database.

Do not include payment card details, passwords or other sensitive information in an enquiry message.

3. WhatsApp and external services

If you choose a WhatsApp number, the website creates a link to WhatsApp with the message you entered. The draft is not sent automatically. Review it and press Send in WhatsApp if you want to contact the hotel. Once you follow that link, WhatsApp and its service providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

This website loads web fonts from Google Fonts and links to or embeds third-party services, which may include Google Maps, WhatsApp, social media and booking platforms. The hosting platform and these services operate independently and may receive technical connection information when your browser loads or opens a page or service. Their own privacy notices apply.

4. Browser storage and basic operation

The site may read a temporary session-storage flag to decide whether an optional introductory film should appear during a browser session. The flag is not a contact detail. The current form flow does not use cookies or browser storage to save the information you type.

The site uses ordinary browser functions to display pages, validate form fields and open the selected external contact destination.

5. Retention and privacy requests

The static website does not retain form entries. If you send a message to HOTEL ND MANOR using WhatsApp, email or another service, the hotel and that service may handle it outside this website. Contact the hotel directly about a message you have sent or a request concerning information shared with it.

For a website privacy question, email ndmanorhotel@gmail.com. The website does not state a retention period for messages held by third-party services.

6. Changes to this policy

This notice may be updated when the website or its enquiry flow changes. The date at the top indicates the latest website revision.
