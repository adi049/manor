Helpful details

Before you enquire.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in and check-out times can change. Confirm your arrival and departure timings directly with the hotel before travelling.

How can I check current room rates?

Contact the hotel for current rates. Any approved rate quote applies to two guests; maximum occupancy is three guests per room. Additional-person charges apply; confirm the amount directly.

Can I ask about a wedding or indoor event?

Yes. Ask about the indoor, air-conditioned banquet hall for weddings, celebrations, family functions and private events. Share the date and approximate guest count; the hotel confirms availability, capacity and arrangements.

Does this form confirm my reservation?

No. It prepares a WhatsApp message draft after you choose a hotel number. You must press Send in WhatsApp; the hotel confirms availability, current rates and booking terms directly.
